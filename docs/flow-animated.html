6 agents: 1 Foundry (evidence) · 4 Copilot Studio (coordinator + Alder, Metro, Riverside) · 1 Fabric data agent. The Activator and pipeline are rules, not agents.

Each step wakes the next. The Caldova app checks every case every 20 s and hands it to whoever acts next. Agents never call each other.

Nothing is booked without step 7. Garages talk by real email (dashed lines), so the thread is the audit trail.
