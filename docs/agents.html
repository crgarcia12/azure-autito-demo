Agents never call each other. The repair worker reads the case state and invokes the next agent. Every agent's answer is validated before the case moves on.

Garage agents work by email. They are triggered by real messages in their own shared mailbox and reply from it. The thread is the audit trail.

Two human gates: the customer's report starts the AI work, and the manager's approval (Copilot's pick or their own, with a reason) is the only thing that books a repair.
